# A1 Curriculum Exam-Preparation Amendments

**Status:** Controlled amendment document. Overlay only — not a curriculum rewrite.

**Source authority:**
1. `a1-final-42-day-curriculum.md` — authoritative, LOCKED A1 language curriculum.
2. `A1_Exam_Coverage_Gap_Map.md` — authoritative exam-coverage audit result.

Where anything below cannot be traced to a specific finding in the Gap Map, it has been left out. This document adds nothing beyond what the Gap Map's Section 4 (Gap Register), Section 5 (Official Material Map), and Section 6 (proposed Exam Preparation Layer) already justify.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Gap Map's verdict (Section 1) is that the 42-day curriculum is **exam-ready for Goethe-Zertifikat A1 specifically**, and **language-ready but task-unfamiliar** for telc Deutsch A1 and ÖSD Zertifikat A1. It identifies exactly four gaps (Section 4), all classified Moderate or Minor, none Critical, and all scoped as **exam-familiarity gaps**, not language-competence gaps.

This document's purpose is narrow: convert those four gaps, and only those four gaps, into precise, minimal amendments that sit on top of the existing Week 6 structure. It does not evaluate whether the curriculum's German is correct, sufficient, or well-sequenced — that question is out of scope and was not reopened.

---

## 2. Amendment principles

These principles govern every amendment below and are restated at the point of use where relevant:

1. **Overlay, not rewrite.** Every amendment is additive (INSERT/ADD) or selective (OVERLAY), never a replacement of existing curriculum text.
2. **No new German.** No amendment introduces new grammar, vocabulary, or communicative functions. The Gap Map is explicit that the language competence already transfers to telc and ÖSD; what's missing is task-format familiarity only.
3. **Days 1–35 are untouched.** All four gaps are located in the Week 6 exam-preparation layer or in a backend QA process; none require reopening earlier weeks.
4. **Goethe remains the default track.** The Gap Map recommends keeping Days 36–41 "exactly as written" (Section 6) because they are well-evidenced and Goethe is what the curriculum was actually built around. telc and ÖSD are added as parallel options, not replacements.
5. **Only verified official materials are referenced.** Every resource named below appears in the Gap Map's Section 5 (Official Material Map) with a confirmed source; nothing is invented.
6. **Traceability.** Section 9 maps every change back to its originating gap.

## 3. Week 6 exam-track selector

**Traces to:** Gap Map Section 6, "Add an optional 'Exam Track Selector' at the start of Week 6."

**What it is:** A single preparatory choice point placed immediately before Day 36, not a lesson and not a language activity.

**Function:** The learner (or the platform, if it already knows the learner's intended exam) indicates one of:

- **Goethe-Zertifikat A1 / Start Deutsch 1**
- **telc Deutsch A1**
- **ÖSD Zertifikat A1**

**What the selector changes:**
- Which short format-bridge notes are surfaced on Days 36–41 (Section 4 below).
- Which official mock material is used on Day 42 (Section 5 below).
- Which timing/scoring figures are displayed alongside the Day 42 mock (Section 7 below).

**What the selector does NOT change:**
- The grammar, vocabulary, or communicative content of Days 36–41, which remain exactly as authored.
- The 42-day structure or day count.
- Anything in Weeks 1–5.

If no selection is made, **Goethe remains the default**, consistent with the curriculum's existing design and the Gap Map's instruction that Days 36–41 stay "exactly as written" for that track.

**Traces to:** Gap Map §5 (Official Material Map) and §6 ("Day 42 becomes multi-track").

**Amendment:** Day 42 retains its existing structure (full mock across all four skills, capstone roleplay, journal entry, mastery check) but the **mock material used** is determined by the Week 6 selector (Section 3 above):

| Track | Official mock material | Confirmed source (Gap Map §5) |
|---|---|---|

The Day 42 capstone roleplay, journal-writing task, and mastery-check standard (Section 5747 area of the curriculum) are **not duplicated or altered per track** — they remain the shared, provider-agnostic capstone described in the existing curriculum, since the Gap Map identifies no exam-specific divergence there.

**Constraint respected:** This does not create three separate Day 42s or extend the calendar. It is one day with a swappable resource pointer, consistent with "Do not add new grammar, vocabulary, or communicative-function content for telc/ÖSD — the evidence shows none is needed" (Gap Map §6).

## 6. Goethe Wortliste QA amendment

**Traces to:** Gap Register Gap 3.

**Amendment type:** A backend/content operation, not a curriculum-day change.

**Specification:**
- **Operation:** One-time comparison of the cumulative curriculum vocabulary (Days 1–42, all thematic vocabulary introduced across Weeks 1–6) against the official Goethe A1 Wortliste PDF (`goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/A1_SD1_Wortliste_02.pdf`, per Gap Map §5).
- **Scope of the comparison:** Coverage verification only — does the curriculum's vocabulary set include every item on the official ~650-word list, and is any confirmed omission a high-frequency, functionally relevant item.
- **This is explicitly NOT permission to:**
  - Add new vocabulary-teaching days.
  - Expand any day's word count "to be safe."
  - Treat the Wortliste as a target to exceed rather than a coverage floor to confirm.
- **If missing words are found:** per the Gap Map's own recommended fix, any genuinely missing high-frequency items should be **folded into existing retrieval/recycling slots** (the curriculum already has a "Retrieval/Recycling" activity in every day's structure) rather than creating new content, new days, or new drills.
- **Why telc/ÖSD are excluded from this QA step:** the Gap Map states explicitly that telc and ÖSD do not publish an equivalent word-count target — this is a Goethe-specific practice, not a repository-wide standard (Gap 3, "Affected exam(s)" line). Running an equivalent check against telc/ÖSD would have no official target to check against and is not proposed.

## 8. DeutschOS implementation requirements

This section describes what the platform needs to know to implement the overlay. No application code is specified, per the task constraint.

The platform needs to be able to determine and persist, per learner:

1. **Exam-track selection state** — one of {Goethe, telc, ÖSD, unselected/default-Goethe} — captured at or before Week 6, editable up to Day 42.
2. **Which official mock resource to launch on Day 42** — a simple lookup from the track selection to the three resources named in Section 5's table (Goethe Modellsatz/Übungssatz; telc Übungstest 1; ÖSD Modellsatz ZA1). No new resource needs to be sourced beyond what the Gap Map has already verified.
3. **Which format-bridge notes to display, and where** — the Section 4 table is effectively a per-day, per-track lookup: (Day, Track) → note text (or no note, for the rows marked "no note" above). This should render only for telc/ÖSD tracks; the Goethe track shows no additional notes since Days 36–41 are already written for it.
4. **Which timing/scoring instructions to display at Day 42** — a per-track lookup from Section 7's table, shown once, at the point the mock is presented.
5. **Whether the learner is using Goethe, telc, or ÖSD** — this single piece of state drives points 2–4 above; it does not need to drive anything else, since Weeks 1–5 and the Day 42 capstone/journal/mastery-check content are explicitly shared across tracks (Section 5).

No other platform state is required by this amendment. In particular, the platform does **not** need per-track versions of Days 1–35, per-track vocabulary lists (beyond the single Goethe Wortliste QA pass in Section 6), or per-track grading logic beyond pointing to the correct official answer key bundled with each track's official mock material.

## 10. Explicit list of what must NOT be changed

- Weeks 1–5 (Days 1–35): no edits, insertions, or reordering of any kind.
- The locked grammar progression (V2 syntax → accusative → dative, etc.) and its week-by-week sequencing.
- The 42-day total structure and day count.
- Any A1 vocabulary, grammar, or communicative-function content, for any track — no new content is added for telc or ÖSD "parity," since the Gap Map found none is needed.
- Days 36–41's existing Goethe-anchored lesson content, objectives, exercises, and Daily-Core connections — these remain exactly as authored; only conditional bridge notes are layered on top for non-Goethe tracks.
- The Day 42 capstone roleplay, interest-driven journal task, and mastery-check criteria — identical across all three tracks.
- Any exam outside the three named as primary (DTZ, DSD I/II, TestDaF, DSH, ECL) — the Gap Map found all of these Not Applicable to this A1 curriculum, and this document introduces no new treatment of them.
- No A2/B1/B2 content of any kind.
