# DeutschOS — 15-Day A0 → Early-A1 Intensive Curriculum

## Day 1 — German foundations and pronunciation

- German sound system and alphabet
- greetings and introductions
- names, origin, residence
- basic pronunciation: ä, ö, ü, ch, r, vowel length, stress
- core expressions for classroom/communication repair
- initial speaking and listening practice

## Day 2 — Personal information and core sentence building

- personal pronouns
- sein
- haben
- present-tense foundations
- simple statements
- yes/no questions
- W-questions
- basic self-introduction
- short personal writing

## Day 3 — Articles, gender and high-frequency nouns

- der/die/das
- ein/eine/einen at introductory level
- grammatical gender
- singular/plural
- noun + article learning
- immediate-environment vocabulary
- object naming and simple sentences

## Day 4 — Main-clause word order and negation

- Verb-second main-clause structure
- sentence patterns
- nicht
- kein
- question formation
- short dialogue practice
- direct German processing with familiar objects/actions

## Day 5 — Numbers, time, dates and daily schedule

- numbers
- age
- clock time
- weekdays
- dates
- basic time expressions
- daily schedule
- listening for numerical information

## Day 6 — Daily routine and common verbs

- high-frequency regular verbs
- selected common irregular verbs
- routine vocabulary
- verb conjugation in context
- speaking about the learner's real routine
- first structured diary entry

## Day 7 — Family and people

- family vocabulary
- occupations/basic personal description
- possessive forms at functional introductory level
- describing familiar people
- listening for personal information
- short descriptive writing

## Day 8 — Home and surroundings

- rooms
- furniture
- common household objects
- haben + everyday descriptions
- basic location expressions
- in/on/at-type location patterns
- describing the learner's room/home

## Day 9 — Accusative foundations

- direct objects
- der → den
- ein → einen
- common accusative verbs such as haben, kaufen, sehen, brauchen
- shopping/object sentences
- controlled production and retrieval

## Day 10 — Food, restaurant and shopping

- food and drink
- restaurant language
- prices and quantities
- möchten
- brauchen
- ordering/requesting
- supermarket roleplay
- menu/price-list reading

## Day 11 — City, directions and transport

- places in town
- left/right/straight ahead
- basic public transport
- asking/giving simple directions
- wo / wohin introduction
- map-based listening and speaking

## Day 12 — Work, school and appointments

- work/course/classroom vocabulary
- basic workplace vocabulary
- appointment language
- asking when/where
- simple scheduling
- practical phone/appointment expressions

## Day 13 — Practical everyday interaction

- requests
- clarification
- communication repair
- asking for help
- basic service interactions
- integrating shopping, transport, appointments and classroom situations
- speaking without reading a script

## Day 14 — Integrated A1 practice

- combine personal information, family, home, routine, shopping, city, work/school and appointments
- mixed listening
- mixed reading
- connected speaking
- diary writing
- retrieval review
- targeted correction of recurring errors

## Day 15 — A0 → early-A1 diagnostic

Assess:

- pronunciation
- listening
- speaking
- reading
- writing
- vocabulary recognition and recall
- core grammar
- direct German processing
- communication repair

Core grammar to assess:

- pronouns
- sein/haben
- present tense
- V2
- questions
- negation
- articles/gender
- plural
- basic possessives
- basic accusative
- core prepositions/location patterns

Diagnostic output should classify each area as:

- Strong
- Developing
- Weak
- Critical

## Important learning principles for every day

- context first, then concise explanation
- translation allowed when genuinely useful, but avoid unnecessary translation
- German → meaning/context should gradually replace German → English/Hindi/Punjabi → meaning
- high-frequency and high-utility vocabulary gets priority
- nouns should normally be learned with article and plural where relevant
- exercises should combine listening, speaking, reading and writing where practical
- real-life context should reinforce the curriculum rather than compete with the Goethe objective
- no advanced B1/B2 grammar should be taught as required mastery
- recurring errors should be recorded for later remediation
- daily real-world implementation, object naming and diary practice should be included
- this document is the canonical source for the first vertical curriculum slice
